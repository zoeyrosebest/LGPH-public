Lithium, 2020

An experimental drama written, directed, shot, and edited by Zoey Rose Best.

Feminism From The Pole, 2017

A short drama written, directed, shot, and edited by Zoey Rose Best.

When Troy threatens to reveal Ivy, who is behind the controversial blog 'From the Pole', she must decide if her anonymity is worth her pride.

Staring Gabi Gambino as Ivy.

A comedic- documentary created and edited by Zoey Rose Best.
